# How announcements are written

These are speaker notes, not a script. The host reads them once and says it in their own words. Write the bones, not the speech.

Approach (Brady Shearer / Pro Church Tools, plus Ben's July 2026 policy): treat each announcement like a pitch. Nobody in the room cares about our event. They care about what it does for them. Sell the outcome, then give one clear next step.

## The shape of every announcement
1. **Hook.** One line that names something the listener wants or feels. A question works.
2. **The offer.** What it is, framed as the payoff to them. Not logistics.
3. **Proof or picture (optional).** A one-line story, a number, a result. Inspiration beats information.
4. **One next step.** One action, one place. Prefer the QR code / Next Steps form. Never two asks.

## Rules
- 3 to 5 bullets. Each bullet under about 12 words. Fragments are good.
- Talk to "you," not about "we." Count the "we"s and cut most of them.
- Only the details someone needs to act: day, time, place. Everything else lives in the bulletin, app or email.
- Write how people talk. Contractions. Short words.
- Banned (church-speak and filler): "we're so excited," "it's that time of year," "opportunity," "don't miss out," "fellowship," "be sure to," "we would love for you to," "partner with us," "exciting things," "as you may know." No exclamation-point stacking. No em dashes.
- Don't explain why the church is doing it. Explain why they'd want to be there.
- Never invent facts (times, prices, rooms, what happens at an event). If a detail isn't in a source, leave it out and flag it to Ben.
- One announcement, one idea. If it needs two asks, it's two announcements or it belongs in the bulletin.

## What qualifies (from Ben's policy)
- Affects at least half the church, OR is a core ministry (Youth, Kids, Father's Table, Worship) and still worth Sunday time.
- Church-wide events and services, big vision/staffing news, Knox County impact, generosity/serve/mission calls for everyone.
- Not: one age group or subgroup events, recurring ministry updates, logistics.
- Timing: announce 1 to 3 Sundays before the thing. Sign-up-driven items go earlier than show-up items.

## Before and after
Before (information):
> We are so excited for Fall Fest next weekend! We have all sorts of fun games, activities, and experiences planned for Saturday night. I encourage you to bring your family and invite a friend with you!

After (value):
- Trick-or-treating, but warmer and way less walking.
- Fall Fest. Saturday, 6 to 8. Games, candy, trunk-or-treat.
- Easiest invite of the year. Your neighbors' kids will thank you.
- Got a creative streak? Enter Best Trunk. QR code in front of you.

Before:
> It's that time of year again! We are excited to offer this great opportunity to serve kids all over the world by collecting Operation Christmas Child boxes.

After:
- One shoebox. One kid. Maybe the only gift they get this year.
- Grab an empty box at the Connection Center on your way out.
- Fill it this week, bring it back by Nov 11.
