# How announcements are written

These are speaker notes, not a script. The host reads them once and says it in their own words. Each bullet should be something a real person would actually say out loud on a Sunday morning.

Approach (Brady Shearer / Pro Church Tools, plus Ben's July 2026 policy): treat each announcement like a pitch. Nobody in the room cares about our event. They care about what it does for them. Lead with the value, then give one clear next step.

## Voice: sound like a person, not a bot
This matters most. Ben wants it to read like a warm, natural speaker wrote it.
- Write in **full, flowing sentences**. Use complex sentences that connect ideas with words like because, so, while, if, when, which, and that. A good bullet is often one or two real sentences.
- **Vary sentence length.** Mix a longer sentence with a shorter one. Never stack fragments.
- **No choppy AI patterns.** Avoid:
  - Strings of fragments or one-word sentences ("Hope. Purpose. What matters most.")
  - Punchy taglines and slogans ("One tag, one kid, one Christmas morning.")
  - Lists of three for rhythm's sake
  - "That's it." / "Here's the thing." / "The best part?"
  - Opening every announcement with a rhetorical question
  - Clipped imperatives with no context ("Grab a card. Invite a friend.")
- Contractions and everyday words. It should sound like Ben or Joe talking, warm and a little conversational.
- No em dashes, anywhere. Use commas, periods, or a semicolon.

## The shape of every announcement
1. **Open with what the listener cares about**: a need, a feeling, or a situation they recognize.
2. **The offer**: what it is, framed as what it does for them.
3. **The details that matter**: day, time, place, in a natural sentence.
4. **One next step**: one action, one place. Prefer the QR code / Next Steps form. Never two asks.

## Rules
- 3 to 4 bullets per announcement. Each bullet is 1 to 2 natural sentences (roughly 15 to 35 words).
- Talk to "you" more than about "we."
- Only the details someone needs to act. Everything else lives in the bulletin, app or email.
- Banned church-speak and filler: "we're so excited," "it's that time of year," "opportunity," "don't miss out," "fellowship," "be sure to," "we would love for you to," "partner with us," "exciting things," "as you may know."
- Don't explain why the church is doing it. Explain why they'd want to be there.
- Never invent facts (times, prices, rooms, what happens at an event). If a detail isn't in a source, leave it out and flag it to Ben.
- One announcement, one idea, one ask.

## What qualifies (from Ben's policy)
- Affects at least half the church, OR is a core ministry (Youth, Kids, Father's Table, Worship) and still worth Sunday time.
- Church-wide events and services, big vision/staffing news, Knox County impact, generosity/serve/mission calls for everyone.
- Not: one age group or subgroup events, recurring ministry updates, logistics.
- Timing: announce 1 to 3 Sundays before the thing. Sign-up items go earlier than show-up items.

## Before and after
Too wordy (information dump):
> We are so excited for Fall Fest next weekend! We have all sorts of fun games, activities, and experiences planned for Saturday night. I encourage you to bring your family and invite a friend with you!

Too choppy (AI):
- Trick-or-treating, but warmer.
- Fall Fest. Saturday, 6 to 8.
- Games. Candy. Trunks.
- Easiest invite of the year.

Right:
- If you're looking for a safe, warm place to take the kids trick-or-treating this year, Fall Fest is next Saturday night from 6 to 8.
- There'll be games, candy, and a trunk-or-treat, and it's one of the easiest things all year to invite your neighbors to.
- If you've got a creative streak, sign up to decorate a trunk using the QR code in front of you, because we're giving a prize for the best one.

Right (Operation Christmas Child):
- For a lot of kids around the world, a shoebox from Operation Christmas Child is the only gift they'll get this year.
- You can pick up an empty box at the Connection Center on your way out today, and there's a guide in the bulletin for what to put inside.
- Fill it with your family this week and bring it back by November 11 so it can ship in time.
